# DOMINUS ULTIMATE — 12 Layers

1. Edge: CDN/WAF/rate limits/DDoS boundary.
2. Application: Next.js/React UI + FastAPI API.
3. Orchestration: Temporal/Airflow-compatible job contracts.
4. Worker Fleet: CPU/GPU workers; provider-backed collection only; no arbitrary shell execution.
5. Integration Fabric: 600 capability contracts across 60+ categories; only configured providers execute.
6. Collection & Feeds: DNS, RDAP, CT, passive DNS, reputation, corporate and other authorized feeds.
7. Enrichment & Normalization: canonical entities, timestamps, source and confidence.
8. Correlation & Knowledge Graph: entities, relationships, clusters, pivots and isolate.
9. Exposure Intelligence: breach/provider feeds and dark-web provider results where licensed; no fabricated or direct illicit access.
10. Silent AI: field-level insight/confidence/reasoning/prediction/anomaly metadata.
11. Polyglot Data: PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Neo4j, ClickHouse, object storage, Redis, Kafka, search and vector stores.
12. Security & Governance: RBAC/ABAC/ReBAC, Vault/KMS, audit, evidence chain, retention and tenant isolation.
