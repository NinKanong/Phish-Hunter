# URL & Domain Analyst V21 Upgrade

V21 extends the canonical runtime with world-scale geography, public-IP geolocation enrichment, country highlight layers, defensive C2/malware-infrastructure triage, infrastructure clustering, analyst coverage scoring and an expanded result dossier.

The map is evidence-led: a highlighted country means the investigation has a provider-returned network/IP geography observation. It does not mean the domain owner or a person is physically located there.
